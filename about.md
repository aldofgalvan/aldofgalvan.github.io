I'm a recent PhD graduate in Mechanical Engineering (Robotics) from UT Austin,
where I worked in the [Human Enabled Robotic Technology Lab (HERO)](https://sites.utexas.edu/herolab/)
advised by Prof. Ann Majewicz Fey. My early research dealt mostly with haptic rendering
and enabling intuitive interactions in simulation. More recently my work has evolved towards
enabling intuitive interactions in real-world teleoperated systems for the purpose of
data collection. Over the course of my PhD I have accumulated extensive experience working
with a diverse array of robotic hardware and software.

Much of my recent work is currently under review — you can learn more about it on my
[Selected Projects](/projects/) page.
