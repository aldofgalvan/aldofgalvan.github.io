Here is an overview of my research, teleoperation systems, and engineering work. Click on any project to view more details, videos, and associated publications.

---

### [Vision-Based Proximity Estimation and Haptic Feedback](/projects/proximity-estimation/)
Extracting spatial proximity purely from stereo endoscopic feeds and retargeting it as directional vibrotactile feedback to reduce cognitive workload during surgical teleoperation.

### [Grasp- and Object-Aware Contact Estimation](/projects/grasp-contact/)
Robust contact estimation that distinguishes between approaching an object and grasping it, remaining reliable under occlusion and dynamic grasping conditions.

### [Contact-Based Skill Assessment](/projects/skill-assessment/)
Deriving metrics from spatiotemporally clustered contact points to characterize human skill in robot-assisted surgery and teleoperation.

### [dVRK Digital Twin & Synthetic Data Generation](/projects/dvrk-digital-twin/)
A digital twin of the da Vinci Research Kit built to support perception model training with domain randomization for robust sim-to-real transfer.

### [Haptic Teleoperation for Robot Learning](/projects/haptic-teleoperation/)
A bilateral teleoperation framework developed with Sony AI for large-scale demonstration collection, enabling real-time, low-latency control and time-aligned HDF5 data export.

### [Task-Space Retargeting for Dexterous Manipulation](/projects/task-space-retargeting/)
A framework enabling dexterous manipulation using a hand exoskeleton with force feedback transmitted via a joint-space virtual coupling control law.
